Our story

Designed with love, care and a lot of intention.

Kuddleroo opened its doors in Chandigarh's Sector 15-D as a milestone we'd imagined for a long time — a room where a toddler's first steps away from home still feel like home.

Every wall here was painted by hand. Trees grow up the corners with birdhouses tucked into their branches. Hot-air balloons drift over a little village. The floors are warm oak, the chairs have bunny ears, and there is a canvas tent for whenever the world feels like a bit much.

It isn't decoration for its own sake. It's an invitation — to look up, to wander over, to ask what that is. That first flicker of curiosity is the whole point.

A place where little learners are encouraged to explore, imagine, and grow at their own pace.

Why the name?

Kuddleroo is inspired by two things we love deeply — kangaroos and cuddles.

🦘

Like a kangaroo's pouch

It stands for warmth, safety and gentle care — somewhere small enough to feel held.

🤗

And just like a cuddle

It's a space where children feel held, loved, and free to grow.

It's a “Toddler's Safe Haven.”

What a session is built on

Four things we protect fiercely.

Every morning at Kuddleroo is designed around these — not worksheets, not milestones on someone else's schedule.

  • Exploration

    Room, permission and time to poke at the world and find out what it does.

  • Communication

    Songs, stories, naming things, and being properly listened to when the words finally come.

  • Motor skills

    Scooping, pouring, gripping, balancing, climbing — small hands and big legs getting stronger.

  • Sensory play

    Grain, water, clay, dough, paint. Learning that goes in through the fingertips first.
